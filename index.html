Filastro "Fil" Mottola Biography

“Filastro Mottola was born in 1915 in Orange, New Jersey, to Italian immigrant parents from Naples. The family moved to Southern California in the early 1920s where young Fil grew up surrounded by the beauty of the Pacific coast that would later become central to his artistic vision.”

He studied at the Otis Art Institute on a scholarship. After serving as a WWII veteran, he joined Walt Disney Studios around 1949–1951.

“From 1949 until 1961, Fil Mottola worked at Walt Disney Studios as a background painter and story sketch artist, contributing to some of the studio’s most beloved animated classics during the golden age of animation.”
“When Disneyland opened in 1955, Walt Disney asked Fil and two other Disney artists to draw visitors on opening day. ‘We spent weeks at Disneyland,’ Fil said. ‘We were out in the open in front of the palace.’ The drawings were $10 each, but the artists were allowed to keep the money.”

Fil Mottola Artistic Style & Legacy

“After leaving Disney in 1961, Fil Mottola devoted himself fully to his fine art, developing a distinctive style that captured the light, color, and atmosphere of the California coast.”

His post-Disney fine art includes luminous coastal scenes such as Fisherman's Wharf, San Francisco, Fisherman's Wharf, Monterey, California, Boats at Morro Bay, and Sparkling Waters, along with Mexican village subjects like Market Place, Taxco, Mexico, and bold postwar abstract works.

“Mottola’s paintings are known for their vibrant saturated colors and expressive brushwork... He achieved huge success painting popular scenes in Santa Barbara in the 1960s and 1970s, and arrived in Laguna Beach in the early 1980s to become its preeminent painter of beach scenes.”

Roberto Luigi Valente (b. 1926)

“Roberto Luigi Valente was born in 1926, the fourth son of a farming and wine producing family of Frosinone, Picinisco, Antica near Cassino in view of the Abruzzi mountains. The Italian coast has been a strong focus in Valente’s work.”

Born in 1926 in the rural village of Antica near Picinisco and Cassino in the province of Frosinone (Lazio, Italy), Roberto Luigi Valente grew up as the fourth son in a farming and wine-producing family. The dramatic landscape—with sweeping views of the Abruzzi mountains, rolling countryside, and proximity to the Mediterranean coast—left a profound and lasting imprint on his artistic sensibility.

This rich Italian heritage deeply informs his vibrant oil (and occasional acrylic) paintings. Valente has famously returned again and again to the light, color, and atmosphere of his homeland for over fifty years. His works capture the luminous quality of the Italian coast, the dramatic bays of Sicily, the historic charm of Venetian canals, the bustling energy of Naples old town, and lively market scenes. The fiery sunsets, azure waters, and earthy tones of the Lazio and southern Italian landscapes recur as central motifs, reflecting both personal memory and a deep emotional connection to place.

Entrepreneurial Endeavors
Valente founded and operated New World Marble Company, supplying premium marble for major California projects including Hearst Castle and numerous celebrity homes. This business allowed him to bridge traditional Italian stone craftsmanship with his life in America.

Artistic Pursuits & Available Works: Valente has exhibited in Rome and Naples. His paintings are held in private collections internationally and appear regularly at auction. Signature subjects include Venetian canals, Sicilian bays, Naples old town views, and market trader portraits.
